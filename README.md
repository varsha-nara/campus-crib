# CampusCrib

CampusCrib is a student housing platform designed by students for students to restore trust, affordability, and safety in the housing search. CampusCrib reduces the housing search crisis for students through three main features:

1. Verified Listings – landlords and listings are reviewed, displaying overcrowding alerts, and scam detection.

2. Roommate Matching – Students can filter based on lifestyle, cleanliness, majors, or even social preferences. Matches are verified through university emails, reducing risk and improving trust.

3. Tenant Education – We include guides on rent transparency, tenant rights, and basic maintenance tutorials—because informed students are harder to exploit.


---

## MVP Features

### Housing Search & Listings
- **Verified listings**: Only accessible via campus email and landlord verification.  
- **Rent transparency**: Rent plus utilities breakdown displayed clearly.  
- **Filters**: Furnished vs. unfurnished, amenities (WiFi, laundry, AC, parking), proximity to campus (walking, biking, shuttle).  
- **Photo & video tours**: Browse spaces remotely.

### Roommate Matching
- **Basic profiles**: Major, year, lifestyle.  
- **Matching filters**: Cleanliness, cooking habits, social/party level.  
- **Verified student accounts**: Only campus emails accepted.  
- **Browse multiple matches**: Compare potential roommates.

### Trust & Transparency
- **Landlord/property reviews and ratings**.  
- **Overcrowding alerts**: Flags unsafe listings.  
- **Tenant rights hub**: Guides on leases, deposits, protections.  
- **Tutorials**: Tenant advocacy, filing complaints, conflict resolution.  
- **Safety checks**: Verified locks, fire codes, lighting.  
- **Pest alerts**: Animal infestations or pest warnings.
